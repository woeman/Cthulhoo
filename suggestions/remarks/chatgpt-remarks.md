## Gentle correction nudges

Use a clue about the category or a discriminating feature, rather than exposing a single answer in a question that accepts many. Reveal a name only when the player asks for the answer or the round is over. Keep the voice playful, never personal.

- “Think of the *kind* of thing the prompt asks for: a place, a person, a title, or an object.”
- “You are in the right subject area; check the era, region, or spelling.”
- “The answer needs to be a specific example, not the broader category.”
- “Try the other name this thing is commonly known by.”
- “You may be thinking of a related item. Check whether it actually fits the prompt’s rule.”
- “For a list question, another valid member of the set may be easier to summon.”
- “Look for the distinguishing detail: who made it, where it came from, or when it existed.”
- “That is close in spirit. The accepted answer belongs to a different branch of the family tree.”

- “The clue points to the material, not the finished object.”
- “Try the plural, singular, or established short name if the fact is right.”
