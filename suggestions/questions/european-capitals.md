# Name the capital city of a European country.

Question id: `european-capitals` · category: geography · source: `data/geography.json`

Rules: The capital of any European country, including Russia, Turkey, Cyprus, Kazakhstan and the Caucasus. Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland count too, as do self-governing islands and territories like the Faroes and Jersey.

The game already forgives capitals, accents, punctuation, a leading "the", simple plurals, "St" for "Saint", number words, a title in front (King, Sir, Dr and the like), the question's own words at the end ("Cheddar cheese"), a year at the end, and small typos (one wrong letter in a short name, more in a long one), so there is no need to suggest those variants.
