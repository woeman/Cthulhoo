# Name an underworld or afterlife from myth or religion.

Question id: `underworlds` · category: myth · source: `data/myth.json`

Rules: Any realm of the dead, heaven, hell or paradise from myth, folklore or religion, ancient or living, by any name. Gods of the dead, the rivers and bridges on the way, and homes of gods that the dead never reach don't count, nor do places made up for novels, short stories, comics, films, TV or games.

The game already forgives capitals, accents, punctuation, a leading "the", simple plurals, "St" for "Saint", number words, a title in front (King, Sir, Dr and the like), the question's own words at the end ("Cheddar cheese"), a year at the end, and small typos (one wrong letter in a short name, more in a long one), so there is no need to suggest those variants.
