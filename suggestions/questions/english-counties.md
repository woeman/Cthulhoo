# Name a county of England.

Question id: `english-counties` · category: geography · source: `data/geography.json`

Rules: Any county of England, past or present: ceremonial, historic, metropolitan or abolished, however long ago. Welsh, Scottish and Irish counties don't count, nor do unitary council areas, nor towns that were once counties of themselves, unless they still are.

The game already forgives capitals, accents, punctuation, a leading "the", simple plurals, "St" for "Saint", number words, a title in front (King, Sir, Dr and the like), the question's own words at the end ("Cheddar cheese"), a year at the end, and small typos (one wrong letter in a short name, more in a long one), so there is no need to suggest those variants.
