# Name a god of the Aztecs, the Maya or the Inca.

Question id: `mesoamerican-gods` · category: myth · source: `data/myth.json`

Rules: Any god or goddess of the Aztecs and other Nahua peoples, the Maya, or the Inca and their Andean neighbours, by any spelling, the divine heroes of their myths included. Kings, conquerors and creatures of later folklore don't count.

The game already forgives capitals, accents, punctuation, a leading "the", simple plurals, "St" for "Saint", number words, a title in front (King, Sir, Dr and the like), the question's own words at the end ("Cheddar cheese"), a year at the end, and small typos (one wrong letter in a short name, more in a long one), so there is no need to suggest those variants.
