# Name a god, spirit or creature of Slavic folklore.

Question id: `slavic-folklore` · category: myth · source: `data/myth.json`

Rules: Any god, spirit, demon or creature from the myths and folklore of the Slavs, from Russia and Ukraine to Poland, the Czech lands and the Balkans, by any spelling. Human heroes of the epics and fairy tales, and creatures made up for modern books and games, don't count.

The game already forgives capitals, accents, punctuation, a leading "the", simple plurals, "St" for "Saint", number words, a title in front (King, Sir, Dr and the like), the question's own words at the end ("Cheddar cheese"), a year at the end, and small typos (one wrong letter in a short name, more in a long one), so there is no need to suggest those variants.
